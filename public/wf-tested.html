What we run

A fixed protocol, stated before the test starts:

  • 3 rolling walk-forward folds. The parameter set is chosen on the in-sample window and run on the next, unseen window.
  • A 9-cell parameter sweep, ranked by expectancy — not by net profit, which selects the cell that fitted the sample best.
  • Real-tick modelling, with commission and swap stressed at 1×, 2× and 3×.
  • The efficiency number, the profitable-fold ratio, the worst losing streak and its location, and the out-of-sample equity curve.
  • A 6-page report you can publish, plus a badge and a public listing.

The integrity rules

  • The seller pays; the seller does not edit. The report is published in full, including a failed result.
  • Right of reply. A seller can attach a response; it is published next to the result, not instead of it.
  • No “approved”, no “profitable”. A pass means the strategy survived this protocol under stated assumptions. It is not a forecast.
  • The conflict is disclosed. Seller-paid, independently run, full report public. Buyers can also request an audit of any demo, and those are published whether the seller likes it or not.
  • Demo or seller-supplied build. No reverse-engineering of a paid product.
