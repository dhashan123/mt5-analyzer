The expensive part of a conversion is not the syntax. It is the behaviour that does not exist on the other platform: the bar-close assumptions, the intrabar stop handling, the session and exchange time, the volume-profile calculation, the difference between a tick and a trade. I map those before writing code, and I tell you which parts will be approximations rather than pretending they are identical.

A conversion starts with a $99 assessment: I read the study or the chartbook, list every behaviour that has to be reproduced, name the ones that cannot be, and quote the build. The assessment is credited against the conversion if you go ahead.
