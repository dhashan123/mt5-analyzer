How to read the efficiency number. Walk-forward efficiency is out-of-sample profit divided by in-sample profit for the same selected parameters. Near 1.0 means the selection generalised. Near 0 means the in-sample result was curve fitting. A negative or undefined value means the method was never in contention - in that case the out-of-sample number is the only one that counts, and it is the one to quote.
