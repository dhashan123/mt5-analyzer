What this means. Walk-forward efficiency is out-of-sample profit divided by in-sample profit for the same selected parameters. Near 1.0 means the selection generalised. Near 0 means the in-sample result was curve fitting. Negative means the selection actively hurt. Here, the parameters that looked best on each training window did not survive the next, unseen window.
