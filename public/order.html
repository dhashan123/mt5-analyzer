How it works

What I will not do

  • Tune your parameters until the numbers look good, and call that a validation.
  • Present an in-sample result as if it were out-of-sample.
  • Quote a number I have not reproduced myself.
  • Promise that your strategy is profitable. I do not know that, and neither does anyone who tells you they do.

Questions

Do you need my source code?
Source is best because the tester needs to compile it with your exact build. An .ex5 works too. If you want an NDA first, say so — see how your code is handled.
How long does it take?
Health check 24 hours. Validation 4 days. Full study 7 days. If the queue is full I will tell you the next available date before you pay.
What if the result is bad?
You get the report, the numbers, and a short call. A negative result that is reproducible is worth more than a positive one that is not. That is why the sample report on this site is a failure.
Can you fix the strategy too?
Yes — that is a separate build or audit, quoted after I see the code. Validation is measurement; it is not parameter tuning.
Is this financial advice?
No. It is a historical simulation with modelled execution, not a forecast or a recommendation.
