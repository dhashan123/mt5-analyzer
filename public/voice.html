What you get

  • The script read in the voice you choose, as a clean MP3.

  • The words back unchanged. If a line reads wrong, say so and I re-record it at no charge.
  • The raw recording and a copy with the text, so you can re-cut it if you need to.

Where it is good for

  • Narration for a course, a product walkthrough, or an explainer video.
  • Voiceover where the pace has to be steady and the delivery has to be even.
  • Reading out a long document: a report, a set of instructions, a manual.
  • Audio for an app, a phone system, or a screen reader.

Where a human voice is the point — a performance, a documentary, something with feeling in it — a synthetic voice is the wrong tool, and I would rather say so than take the order and disappoint you.
