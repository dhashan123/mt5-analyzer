What this tells you that a screenshot does not

A Strategy Tester report is a page of numbers. The ones that decide whether a strategy is worth trading are the ones MT5 does not print: the equity curve rebuilt from the deals, the worst losing streak and where in the sample it happened, the break-even win rate implied by your average win and average loss, profit split by day-of-week and hour-of-day, and what the result looks like when commission and swap are doubled and tripled.

This page computes all of them, and gives you a plain reading of the result.

How to get your report

In MetaTrader 5: open the Strategy Tester, set it up, press Run, then right-click the Results grid → Report → HTML. It is written to your terminal's data folder. If you already have one, this page is the fastest way to read it.

Why the sample report is a failure

The sample walk-forward report on this site lost money out of sample: +8,258.81 in sample, −3,398.03 out of sample, efficiency −0.41. It is published in full because a report that only ever confirms what you hoped is worth nothing. Read it before you pay anyone.

The trading tools

Three MetaTrader 5 utilities on the MQL5 Market, written by the same person who wrote this page. Each one solves one specific problem and none of them places a trade for you.

Open Risk Board 39 USD, or rent from 30 A board on your chart that answers what you have at risk right now: the money in every open position, the total against your balance, the R-multiple on each trade, and the size the next trade should be for the risk you chose. It reads the account and never changes a position. Position Exit Manager 49 USD, or rent from 30 Puts a trailing stop and a break-even on every open position, moves them as the trade moves, and can close a set of positions together when something has gone wrong with the account rather than with one trade. Tp and Sl for all open trades 30 USD Sets the take profit and the stop loss on every open trade at once, so a position opened on one chart is not left without protection while you work on another. It works on accounts that are hedging or netting.

There is a free demo of the Exit Manager, so you can see it on your own account before you decide: Position Exit Manager Panel.

Privacy
