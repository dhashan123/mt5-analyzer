About

I am Dhashan Naidoo. I build MetaTrader 5 tools, validate trading systems, and publish the failures as well as the passes.

Why the sample report is a failure

Most EA marketing shows an equity curve that goes up. That is the easy part: with enough parameters, any history can be fitted. The useful question is what happens on data the system was not optimised on. The sample report is a real strategy with a positive in-sample result and a negative out-of-sample result — efficiency −0.41. It is published in full because a validator who only shows winners is selling, not measuring.

What I will not promise

No guaranteed returns, no “make it pass the challenge” tuning, no copying a paid product, and no claim that a backtest is a forecast. A backtest is evidence about the past; the report says how strong that evidence is and where it breaks.
