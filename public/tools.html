MetaTrader 5 tools

Three utilities for MetaTrader 5, by the same person. Each one does a single job on the chart. None of them places a trade for you.

Open Risk Board 39 USD, or rent from 30 Shows the money at risk in every open position, the total against your balance, and the size the next trade should be for the risk you chose. It reads the account and never changes a position. Position Exit Manager 49 USD, or rent from 30 A panel over every open position with a trailing stop, a break-even and a manual exit, and a way to close a set of positions together when the account is the problem rather than one trade. Tp and Sl for all open trades 30 USD Sets the take profit and the stop loss on every open trade at once, so a position opened on one chart is not left unprotected while you work on another.

There is a free demo of the Exit Manager, so you can see it on your own account before you buy anything.

The Risk Board

The question it answers is one the chart does not: how much can this account lose from here, and how big should the next trade be?

It draws a table of every open position with the distance to its stop loss, the money that distance represents, and the R-multiple of the trade so far, so a position that has already run three times its risk looks different from one that has not. Under the table it shows the total at risk against the balance, and the lot size that would put a chosen percentage of the balance at risk at the current stop distance.

It reads the account and it never changes a position. It has no order button and it sends nothing.

The Exit Manager

Every open position gets a row: the ticket, the type, the lot, the open price, the current price, the profit, and the stop loss and take profit that are on it. You can move the stop, set a break-even, set a trailing stop, close one position, or close a set of them together.

The reason it exists is that a position opened on one chart and then worked on from another is a position nobody is watching. The Exit Manager puts them all in one place, so the account is visible at a glance and a single action can be taken across it.

Tp and Sl for all open trades

Sets the take profit and the stop loss on every open trade at once, from one place, rather than one chart at a time. It works on hedging and on netting accounts, it can apply the same levels to everything or leave the levels alone and only show them, and it can refuse to move a stop that would put the trade into a loss.

What none of them do

None of these is a strategy, and none of them will tell you that a trade is a good one. They are tools for managing risk and exits on positions you have already opened, and they do not open positions.

No claim of profitability is made anywhere on this site or in any of these products. Any number a backtest shows is a historical simulation with modelled execution, not a forecast.
